Hi, I'm Alina

The human behind Pigeon Polly

I'm a learning experience designer with over six years in digital education. I design online courses and integrate AI into the learning process, from course production to everyday teaching workflows.

I'm also doing a master's in Creative Industries and Growth Management, exploring how culture shapes the creative economy.

And I paint. The drawing here is the very first Pigeon Polly, made while I was writing my thesis. Studying and drawing have gone hand in hand ever since, and this lab is where they meet.
