    Work in progress · Book 3

    Urban Sketching
    Digital Traveler

    I'm writing my third sketching guide. This time it's about drawing cities you can't (or don't want to) walk into right now: street panoramas, photos and videos, and a sketchbook on your own kitchen table.

    Perspective made simple, weather, people and pigeons, color accents, composition, references and a lot of stories.

    Every illustration is made by hand, without AI. Coming soon on Amazon.

    Everyone who signs in gets a bird

    Our flock

    Everyone who has ever signed in on this site lives here as a little pixel bird. No two birds are alike: different shapes, hats and very fancy shoes. Every 10th guest is a friendly cat, every 11th a crow with a treasure in its beak. Click a nickname to see the profile.
