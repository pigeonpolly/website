    Urban Sketching
    Digital Traveler

    I'm writing my third sketching guide. This time it's about drawing cities you can't (or don't want to) walk into right now: street panoramas, photos and videos, and a sketchbook on your own kitchen table.

    Perspective made simple, weather, people and pigeons, color accents, composition, references and a lot of stories.

    Every illustration is made by hand, without AI. Coming soon on Amazon.

    Your own pixel character

    Get a bird and join the flock

    Everyone who signs in on this site gets their own little pixel character. It is drawn from your account, so no two are alike: different shapes, colours, hats and very fancy shoes.

    1. Sign in with Google and choose a nickname. Your character appears in the flock right away.
    2. Draw in the daily challenge to collect badges and keep your streak going.
    3. Open gifts in your bag: hats, scarves, backgrounds, frames and treasures. Wear them or keep them for later.
