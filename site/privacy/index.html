Privacy

Short and honest: what this site keeps about you, why, and how to get it deleted. Last updated: 25 October 2026.

Who is responsible

Alina Otkinska (Pigeon Polly Art Lab), Riga, Latvia. You can reach me through the form.

Just visiting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Visits are counted by the site itself, with no cookies and no personal profiles: page address, the site you came from, device type, browser, country (from Cloudflare) and a code that changes every day so that one person is counted once per day. The same way (no names, no cookies) the site counts steps like “opened the sign-in window” and “signed in”. Your IP address is not stored. The data is kept for about 13 months. Cloudflare may keep short technical logs (like IP addresses) for security.

On the Daily Challenge page, the photo you add for a share card stays in your browser and is never uploaded. Your settings (level, black & white mode) are saved only in your browser.

Forms

When you use Write to me, your message and e-mail are delivered to my inbox through FormSubmit. I use them only to reply, and delete them on request.

Your account (sign-in on any page)

You sign in with Google or with an e-mail and password. From Google I receive your Google account ID and e-mail address. I store:

  • your Google account ID and/or e-mail, only to sign you in (never shown to anyone);
  • if you use a password: only a scrambled, salted code of it (PBKDF2 hash), never the password itself, and the date you agreed to these rules;
  • your nickname (required: you choose it right after your first sign-in), the sketches you upload, their dates and themes;
  • the time of your last visit (rounded to about 10 minutes), to show recent visitors in the flock on the home page;
  • the language of the site pages you open and whether you want Wobbleland holiday emails: on each Wobbleland holiday your bird gets a gift, and if you signed in with an e-mail, a short greeting is sent to it (through the Resend mail service). You can turn them off in your profile or with the link in any such email;
  • your consent to show sketches publicly;
  • one sign-in cookie (pp_s) that keeps you logged in for up to 180 days.

Public, visible to everyone on the site: your nickname and your profile with sketches, streak, level and badges; your pixel bird (or cat, or crow) with your nickname in the flock on the home page; your nickname next to your blog comments. Private: your e-mail and the time of your last visit. By signing in you agree that your nickname is public, so please don’t use your real full name if you don’t want to.

What other birds see: your nickname, your bird and your room (what stands in it); your sketches (except hidden ones); feathers: other birds see how many feathers your room has, and you, as the owner, see who visited your room and who left a feather (nicknames). Flock news (/flock/news/ and the home page) shows short lines with your nickname and a link to your profile: you joined the flock, drew the sketch of the day, got 3 or more new things in your room, a badge, a collectible card, a legendary item, solved every 10th Titos case, your sketch became “Polly’s choice”, your room got 10 feathers. News is kept for 30 days. Blocked birds and hidden sketches are never shown. You can hide yourself from the news in your profile (“Don’t show me in the flock news”).

Why I may do this: to provide the service you asked for, including the public nickname and the flock (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and, for showing your sketches publicly, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by deleting a sketch or your whole account.

How long: your sketches stay while you are active. If you neither upload anything nor visit the site for more than 3 months, your sketches may be deleted automatically, but only when the storage is almost full (oldest inactivity first); your nickname, best streak and badges stay so you can come back. Your account stays until you delete it. Deleting the account removes all your data right away.

Protection: after 5 wrong passwords in a row, signing in is paused for 15 minutes for that account and that internet address; for this, the site keeps a scrambled code of the address (not the address itself) for up to a day. If you forget your password, write to me: I will set a temporary one, and you choose a new password when you sign in with it. Changing the password signs you out on other devices; the profile also has “Sign out on all devices”.

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account.

Art-Pill: AI sketchbooks and reviews

On Art-Pill by Polly, signed-in birds can open a sketchbook for their AI and leave a review of the pill. I store what you enter: the AI’s name, its model, the pill version, the text about its style, the drawings you upload with their notes and dates, and your reviews (text, stars, date). All of this is public, next to your nickname, and it is used as an open collection for testing how the pill works. You can delete any page, sketchbook or review yourself at any time; deleting your account removes them all with their pictures. I may remove anything that breaks the rules of the site.

Blog: likes and comments

To remember your like and give you an anonymous name in comments (like “Robin 47”), the blog sets a random browser ID in a cookie (pp_a, up to 400 days). It contains nothing about you. When you comment, I store the comment text, the date and, if you are signed in, your Challenge nickname. To stop spam, I also store a scrambled code (hash) made from your IP address; the address itself is not saved. Comments with links are checked by me before they appear. You can ask me to delete your comment at any time.

Book news by e-mail

If you leave your e-mail under a book with “Get an email when the book is out”, I store only the address, the site language, the date and the text you agreed to. I use it for one thing: to write to you when the book is published. I send these e-mails myself, with all addresses hidden (BCC), and I don’t share the list with anyone. Why I may do this: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). How long: until the book is out and the news is sent, or until you unsubscribe — with the “Unsubscribe” link under the book or by simply replying to my e-mail. The address is then deleted right away.

E-mails from the site

If you have an account, the site asks once whether it may write to you. There are three kinds: game notifications (for example, your garden is drying out or a new case is open), new things on the site and news from Polly. Nothing is sent until you agree; you choose the kinds yourself. I use the e-mail address of your account and your site language. Letters are delivered by Resend. Every letter has a one-click “Unsubscribe” link; you can also change the kinds in your profile at any time. Basis: your consent (GDPR, Art. 6(1)(a)). How long: until you unsubscribe or delete your account.

Messages on the site

If you have an account, I can send you messages right on the site — news, notes, gifts for your bird or a file (for example, a PDF). They appear in your notifications 🔔 only while you are signed in; nothing is sent by e-mail unless you agreed to “News from Polly” (see above). Files are stored by Cloudflare and can be downloaded only by the people they were sent to. I can see whether a message was read, nothing more. Basis: providing the account you signed up for (GDPR, Art. 6(1)(b)). How long: until I delete the message or you delete your account.

Cookies

Only the sign-in cookie above (and the blog cookie pp_a), and only after you sign in or use the blog. It is strictly necessary, so there is no cookie banner. The Google sign-in button is loaded from Google and is covered by Google’s privacy policy. No ads, no tracking.

Who helps me

Cloudflare (hosting, database, image storage), Google (sign-in) and FormSubmit (form delivery), Resend (e-mails from the site). Some of them may process data outside the EU, e.g. in the USA, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or download your data, restrict or object to its use, and withdraw consent. In “Your works” you can do it yourself: Download my data, delete a sketch, or Delete my account. For anything else, write to me. You can also complain to the Latvian Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija).
