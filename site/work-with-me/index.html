services

Work with me

I help schools and teams build learning that works, bring AI into everyday teaching, and I teach adults to sketch. If one of these sounds like your project, write to me.

Learning design & online courses

From a raw syllabus to a course people actually finish: structure, materials, assessment and a clear experience in Moodle or another LMS.

  • Course and curriculum design
  • Moodle setup and course architecture
  • Onboarding and training for teachers

AI in education

Practical AI for course teams: automated production pipelines, AI assistants for staff, and training on how to use them well and safely.

  • AI course-production pipelines
  • AI support tools and assistants for teams
  • Workshops on AI for educators

Sketching workshops & illustration

Watercolor and ink sketching for adults who want to start, not to be perfect. And illustrations with character.

  • Watercolor sketching course (20 hours)
  • Workshops for teams and events
  • Illustrations and characters

How it works

  1. Write to me. Tell me about your idea in a few lines.
  2. We talk. A short call to see if we’re a good fit.
  3. Plan. Scope, timeline and price, with no surprises.
  4. Make it. Step by step, with regular check-ins.
