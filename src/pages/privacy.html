Privacy

Short and honest: what this site keeps about you, why, and how to get it deleted. Last updated: 4 October 2026.

Who is responsible

Alina Otkinska (Pigeon Polly Art Lab), Riga, Latvia. You can reach me through the form.

Just visiting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. It counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and builds no personal profiles. Cloudflare may keep short technical logs (like IP addresses) for security.

On the Daily Challenge page, the photo you add for a share card stays in your browser and is never uploaded. Your settings (level, black & white mode) are saved only in your browser.

Forms

When you use Write to me or Send my sketch to the wall, your message, e-mail, nickname and photo are delivered to my inbox through FormSubmit. I use them only to reply or to choose sketches for the wall, and delete them on request.

Sketch Wall account

You sign in with Google. From Google I receive your Google account ID and e-mail address. I store:

  • your Google account ID and e-mail, only to sign you in (never shown to anyone);
  • your nickname, the sketches you upload, their dates and themes;
  • your consent to show sketches publicly;
  • one sign-in cookie (pp_s) that keeps you logged in for up to 180 days.

Public: your nickname, sketches, streak and level. Private: your e-mail.

Why I may do this: to provide the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and, for showing your sketches publicly, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by deleting a sketch or your whole account.

How long: until you delete your sketches or your account. Deleting the account removes your data, sketches and streak right away.

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account.

Cookies

Only the sign-in cookie above, and only after you sign in. It is strictly necessary, so there is no cookie banner. The Google sign-in button is loaded from Google and is covered by Google’s privacy policy. No ads, no tracking.

Who helps me

Cloudflare (hosting, database, image storage), Google (sign-in) and FormSubmit (form delivery). Some of them may process data outside the EU, e.g. in the USA, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or download your data, restrict or object to its use, and withdraw consent. On the Sketch Wall you can do it yourself: Download my data, delete a sketch, or Delete my account. For anything else, write to me. You can also complain to the Latvian Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija).
