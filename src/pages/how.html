the three words you need

In one minute

🐦

Your bird

A tiny pixel pigeon, yours alone. It appears when you sign in and pick a nickname, free, in about a minute. It walks around its room, wears hats and scarves, holds a doughnut in its beak and lives in the Flock with the other birds.

🔘

Buttons

The only money here, and you cannot buy them. You earn them: +3 for the first visit of the day, +10 for a drawing in the daily challenge, and more for games, the garden, small deeds and invitations. Buttons are for birds: you need to be signed in to collect them.

🛍

The bird market

The shop, but only for bird things: hats, scarves, boots, frames, backgrounds, furniture, wallpaper, seeds and cards. Prices are in buttons. Stock is limited, so the price goes up when something is almost gone.

a small dictionary

What the words mean

Bird
Your pixel pigeon. Polly is the painted character from the paintings and books; your bird is a small pixel cousin from the same world.
Flock
All the birds live on one wall: the Flock. Visit someone’s room and leave a feather.
Buttons
The site money. Earned for being here, drawing and playing; never bought with a card.
Bird market
The shop for your bird and its room. Nothing is posted: everything is pixel, and it stays on the site.
Bag
Where bought and gifted things wait. From the bag they go onto the bird or into the room.
Room
The bird’s pixel room: wallpaper, floor, a view from the window, curtains, furniture, decorations. There are six rooms in the house; some open a little later.
Garden
Behind the house. Plant seeds, water every day, catch bugs and sell the harvest for buttons.
Daily challenge
A new theme every day: draw it, upload it and see it on the wall. A drawing a day keeps the streak alive.
Cards
Collectible pixel cards. Each exists in a single copy on the whole site, so the one in your bag is the only one like it.
Shift with Polly
A focus timer with a pixel office. Work next to Polly, and every full hour brings a promotion and buttons.

honest answers

If you’re wondering…

Do I have to sign in?

No. Lessons, the journal, books, palettes, wallpapers and the challenge wall are open to everyone. A bird needs a sign-in: Google, or an email and a password. Both are free.

Is it really free?

Yes. Buttons cannot be bought and nothing on the site is sold for money. My books are on Amazon — that’s a separate, real-world shelf.

Will a parcel arrive?

No. The bird market is a pixel shop: hats, furniture and cards for your bird. Nothing is posted.

Will you write to me?

Only if you ask. There is a note when a new book comes out — you can sign up for it on the home page. Letters from the Lab (news, garden reminders, a new case) have their own switches in your profile, and you can turn them off at any time.

What if I skip a week?

Nothing disappears. The bird waits, the garden may need water, the streak starts again from one — the buttons you earned stay yours.

How much time does it take?

Two minutes for a daily visit, five to fifteen for a drawing. There is no timer and nothing to lose.
