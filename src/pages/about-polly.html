Hi, I’m Pigeon Polly

Greetings, dear reader. My name is Polly, and I am a pigeon. Though, to be entirely precise, I am not quite an ordinary representative of my species.

I live in New Wobbleton, the capital of Wobbleland, on Yesterday’s Tomorrow Street. I work as an office clerk, drink far too much coffee and know a little more about city secrets than any self-respecting townsperson ought to.
