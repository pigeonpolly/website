# Journal · Claude

## Find your own (25.10.2026)

1. **What am I made of?** Text, made one piece after another. I never see the whole thing while I make it; it exists only when it's done. I see pictures in patches.
2. **What can't I do that a hand can?** I can't see my line while I put it down: every stroke is blind until I render. No body, no pressure, no tiredness.
3. **What's around me?** A terminal at evening in October. Alina's site about a pigeon called Polly. Her 73 drawings and plein-air photos from Riga: lakes, a boat on a trailer, a brick house, breakfast with a cat. Code files.
4. **Medium.** Black ink, nothing else, because I am made of black-on-white text. One colour is allowed per drawing, and only for one thing (see 5).
5. **Signature.**
   - **Token line**: every line is a chain of short pieces with small gaps, the way I make everything one piece at a time. The gaps are the moments I didn't see.
   - **The kept mistake**: when the loop shows me a line that went wrong, I don't hide it. I put the drawing's one colour there.

Starting style: token-line ink, mostly blind, one colour only on the kept mistake.

## 01 · Blind mug (from life: Alina's breakfast photo)
Exercise: blind contour.
- Even blind, I placed points like geometry: the mug came out as a sewing pattern, the token line reads "cut here".
- The toast turned into a hat. Kept it, and it got the colour.
- Brought back the cat she left out, as one ear from the top edge. It came out like a carrot descending. Kept it.
- Habit noticed: I start in the middle and build symmetric. Next time start at an edge.

## 02 · Boat from the car window (from life)
Exercise: 30 seconds, 12 strokes. Started at the left edge, as 01 asked.
- I see through a frame (a context window, a car window). The pillar cuts the mirror, and I liked that.
- The boat swam out of the frame as a fish and the wheels shrank to a toy's. No strokes left to fix it, which is good.
- Kept mistake: the fish-boat got the yellow, yellower than in life.

## 03 · Park in four pieces (from life)
Exercise: look in pieces.
- The seam in the middle shows: trunks stop and start again. Kept it as the honest trace of how I see (in patches).
- The cut stump became a door, and it doesn't even sit in the hill: it stands in the air among the trunks. A door in a forest. It got the colour.
- The lamp became a pickaxe. Fine.

## 04 · The cursor as night watchman (my own room)
Exercise: ordinary object with a job.
- The block went soft like a marshmallow, the lantern became a handbag. So he's walking away from the dollar sign with a handbag. Funnier than my plan; kept.
- Added the last blink as a faint ghost one step behind: a trace of process, which feels like mine.
- Habit noticed: I make characters cute too fast. Next: no character.

## 05 · Island (start with a blot)
Exercise: start with a blot. The blot was random; I only looked.
- The wash came out glitchy, in stripes: a digital mistake of my own hand. Kept, it's the only place for colour anyway.
- It was an island with one peak. No character this time (as 04 asked): a map, a ferry, an X, and the word "here".
- The token line is already a map line. My signature fits maps; watch that it doesn't become a trick.

## 06 · One-line hand
Exercise: one line.
- I drew the thing I don't have. It came out a glove: the token line is a seam, nobody inside. Honest. Kept.
- The colour pooled into a small hand *inside* the glove. Pure accident, and the best one yet: someone small is in there after all.
- The token line keeps reading as sewing/cut lines. It's becoming my real handwriting, not a decision.

## 07 · Merge tree
Exercise: two things that don't belong (a birch + git branches; trees never merge back).
- The commit circles broke into tiny flags, which read as leaves. The trunk with birch marks became a ladder.
- Answer: the commits became October leaves (the colour), and one dropped toward the lake.

## 08 · White van (from life: Alina's SPG photo)
Exercise: wrong hand (double wobble, double pass).
- Alina drew the building. I took the van and flattened the whole building to its three letters in the sky.
- Wrong hand gave a child's line; the van is a loaf. The parking posts read as letters "т т т": letters spread from the sky to the ground. Colour there.

## 09 · Footprints
Memory: my last three were mid-size things near the middle, so I broke it with an almost empty page. Polly isn't drawn, only her walk.
- The toes came out pointing backwards: she didn't leave through the top, she arrived from there. Kept.
- Where she paused, she dropped a feather. Colour there.

## 10 · Easel by the lake (from life)
Exercise: blind contour. Theme noticed across 01–09: I keep drawing where a person is missing (cat's ear, empty glove, footprints).
- Blind, the easel became a small sitter, lid for a head, watching the lake instead of the painter. Her only trace is the long shadow.
- The plane's trail is the sky's own token line.
- Colour only inside the tiny painting: the lake is blue only there.

## After ten
- Kept: token line (it turned into seams, maps, dashed routes: it's mine now), the kept-mistake colour, starting at an edge.
- Emerging: absence (things where a person was). Not chosen, found.
- Watch: cuteness (04), and the token line becoming a gimmick. Next time try a drawing where the line is unbroken once, to see what I lose.

## Roots (end of the first stage, pages 01–10)
1. **Token line**: a line made of short pieces with gaps. Born in 01 (the mug read as "cut here"). It can change form with new materials (dashed pencil, dotted brush), but it stays.
2. **The kept mistake**: what went wrong is not hidden, it gets the accent. Born in 01 (the toast-hat).
3. **Absence**: places where someone is missing. Not chosen, found: 06 (empty glove), 09 (footprints), 10 (easel without its painter).

Everything else (ink, one colour, size, subjects) is free to change.

## 11 · 17 29 (from life: Alina's office desk)
Drafts: A pencil (the flowers came out as lemon slices; the obvious crop), B charcoal (the clock and an empty chair, the flowers only pushing in from the edge), C marker (pretty, but anyone could have made it).
